Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Summary

Lararium is a family tree app. Your family data is stored on your device first. You can use every core feature — building trees, editing people, exporting files, and memorial tributes — without an account and without an internet connection.

2. Data we store

  • On your device (always): the family tree data you enter — names, dates, places, photos you attach, notes, and memorial messages you write. This stays in the app's local database until you choose to enable cloud sync.
  • In the cloud (only if you sign in and enable sync): a copy of your tree data and the media you upload, stored on Supabase (we can host it in the EU region) so your family can collaborate across devices.
  • Account data (only if you sign in): your email address and authentication identifiers.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertising that builds a profile about you.
  • We do not access your contacts, microphone, or precise location. Photos are only read when you explicitly attach them.

4. Living people's privacy

Information about living people in your trees is private by default and is never shown to users outside your family tree. Public memorial pages (if you enable them) only show the deceased person's name, life dates, and memorial content.

5. User-generated content

Memorial messages written by family members are user-generated content. Messages are visible only to members of that family tree by default. You can report messages inside the app; tree owners can delete or hide messages. We may remove content that violates our terms.

6. Deletion

  • Local data: deleting the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Account & cloud data: in the app: Settings → "Delete my account & data" (available once signed in), plus a web deletion form. Deletion removes your account and all cloud copies of your trees.

7. Children

Lararium is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: use the in-app entry or the contact channel listed on the Google Play store page.
